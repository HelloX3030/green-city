How it works

Sag's Heilbronn · greencity.parsalotfi.com
01 · MELDEN

Report

Three taps, location from GPS. What bothers you here?

Too loudToo hotBad airNo shadeLitter*Broken road*Other
* Outlook: the same loop works for anything a city can measure.
02 · BÜNDELN

Cluster

Reports within 100 m form one spot. The number on the map is the number of reports.

03 · PRÜFEN

Check

Each spot is compared with the nearest sensor; shade against the city’s tree register.

BestätigtNicht bestätigtSensor fehlt
Where the sensor is missing, reports decide: “Platz 1 von 8 Stellen ohne Sensor” (no. 1 of 8 spots without a sensor) – that is where the next sensor goes.
What exists today
  • The app, live at greencity.parsalotfi.com.
  • Data from openSenseMap (senseBox:home stations and senseBox:bike rides) and the Umweltbundesamt station DEBW015.
  • The city’s noise map 2022 and tree register as map layers.
  • Our own sensor “NoiseNode”: senseBox MCU-S2 + sound level meter, 3D-printed case. Live on openSenseMap since 28 October 2026, measuring LAeq, Lmax and loud events per minute. No audio is recorded.
  • Open source on GitHub: github.com/HelloX3030/green-city
The ask

The next 10 NoiseNodes for the streets of the Lärmaktionsplan – measured before and after the planned low-noise asphalt.

So the city has a measured answer in 2027 instead of a recalculation in 2029.
