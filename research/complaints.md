## 3. Stage snippet

1. "67 % of Germans are annoyed by road noise at home, 58 % by neighbours." (UBA 2024)
2. "Two thirds say heat periods burden their health; 84 % want better heat protection." (UBA Umweltbewusstsein 2024, n = 2,552)
3. "In Heilbronn 40,100 people live above 55 dB road noise, 16,100 above 65 dB; the city estimates 12,450 strongly annoyed." (Lärmkartierung 2022)
4. "Heilbronn is 54 % sealed: worst heat score in Baden-Württemberg, 2nd worst of 190 German cities." (DUH Hitze-Check 2024)
5. "The city's noise consultation got 36 answers. Its Mängelmelder got 9,000 reports in 3 years, but has no noise, heat or air category." (DS 208/2024; Stadtzeitung 27.11.2024)
6. "Citizens whose first report got a response are 57 % more likely to report again." (Sjoberg/Mellon/Peixoto, PAR 2017, 399,364 FixMyStreet users)

## 4. Heilbronn findings (verified)

- Lärmaktionsplan IV: 23 submissions (Sep–Oct 2023) + 13 (Apr–May 2024) = 36; decided 7 Oct 2024 (DS 208/2024). Tempo 30 on Frankenbacher and Theodor-Heuss-Str. came from citizen input. Round III (2018/19): 19 + 3.
- 2024 submissions named Allee, Frankenbacher, Haller, Heidelberger, Ludwigsburger, Lutzstr., Sontheimer, Stuttgarter Str. City reply: Poser noise and Gewerbelärm are outside the EU noise directive.
- Noise hotspots (DS 223/2023): Neckartalstr., Paulinenstr., Wilhelm-Leuschner-Str., Mannheimer/Weinsberger, Karlsruher/Weststr., Wilhelmstr., Neckarsulmer Str. Stadtbahn and industry: no action need. 16,300 residents at Lnight >= 55 dB.
- Mängelmelder (since Nov 2020): ~9,000 reports in 3 years, ~20/day, 13 categories, ~3,600 each "Müll und Abfall" and "Straße und Verkehr" (S6). echo24 06.08.2023: >2,100 public reports, >920 waste; hotspot Innenstadt Südstr./Oststr./Mannheimer Str. (S7). Totals conflict.
- DUH 2024: sealing 54.34 %, green volume 1.96 m³/m², red card; only Ludwigshafen worse (S9, S10).
- Klimaanpassungskonzept (June 2025): priority zone Hauptbahnhof–Allee–Mannheimer Str.–Rollwagstr.; unsealing of Marktplatz, Hafenmarkt, Kiliansplatz. No citizen-survey numbers published (S11).
- Air: NO2 limit historically exceeded at Weinsberger Str. (Luftreinhalteplan 2020); current values unverified.
- Unverified: hot days/year in Heilbronn; UBA 2024 figures taken from IÖW slides (Kurzbericht PDF 404).
