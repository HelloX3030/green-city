# app

SvelteKit + MapLibre. One page: map, one bottom sheet (report, cluster card, sensor card, layers).

Look: CARTO Positron basemap, glass surfaces, one green for actions. Colours and icons per report type, sensor kind and layer live in `src/lib/api.ts`, the icon paths in `src/lib/icons.ts`. Sensors and clusters are DOM markers (`.pin` styles in `Map.svelte`), trees, bike rides and the bubble discs are map layers.

One radius: 100 m (`CLUSTER_RADIUS_M` in `api.ts`, `RADIUS_M` in the backend) is the only circle in the app, always in metres on the ground. The report circle is it, every bubble's disc is it (faint from zoom 15, the selected one with a line and the single reports from 15.5), and the join rule is it: a report goes to the nearest bubble whose anchor is within 100 m, else it opens a new one. Anchors are the oldest report and never move, so bubbles stay put. Bubble size grows with the log of the weight (one voice per person and day, halves every 7 days), 30 px for one voice and 64 at most, a bit smaller below zoom 14. Smaller pins sit above bigger ones, sensors (28 px) included, so nothing hides under a big bubble; the selected pin is on top. Below zoom 14 bubbles that would overlap on screen merge into one at the heaviest one's spot (a second ring marks it), reports and voices summed, coloured by the type with most reports in the group; a tap zooms in until they come apart. The number is the reports, the colour is the type most people name (one voice per person and day), not the last tap. The card headline is the same number ("12 Meldungen"), below it the people ("von 8 Personen", or "Du und 3 andere"), the type and how many reports are from this week. With more than one type, or when you reported here, the card has a chip per type (icon and reports), the types you named ringed in their colour; a tap filters the texts. The texts are the reports with a message, yours first with a "Du" tag, then newest first, all of them, no author; the input says they are public. The sheet is at most 72 % of the screen high and scrolls inside. On a bubble you reported to today the button reads "Nochmal melden"; a second report today is saved and counted like any other, it just does not grow the bubble (one voice per person and day, explained in Über).

Title pill top left opens a short about sheet (three steps, status colours). Layout: one bottom sheet on phones, a 400 px card bottom-left from 600 px width, the map centre shifts away from it.

Reporting: Melden flies to the phone's position at zoom 16 if it comes within 2 s, else zooms to 15 where you are. Below zoom 15 the circle is grey and Melden disabled. The bubble the report would join gets a green ring and the hint says so, computed in the app with the backend's rule. Position denied or outside Heilbronn shows a toast. The map padding waits for a running animation, setPadding would cancel it.

Live: the app listens on `GET /events` (server-sent events) and refetches clusters and its own reports whenever the server says reports changed, so a report from another phone shows up within a second or two. While the stream is up the clusters poll runs once a minute, without it every 5 s (that is also how "Server nicht erreichbar" is noticed). Coming back to the tab refetches everything.

Identity: no login. The app keeps a random token in localStorage (`deviceToken` in `api.ts`) and sends it as `X-Device` on every request, the backend turns it into a user on first contact. Clearing site data means a new user. The server says per bubble how many reports are mine: those bubbles get a green dot and the card says "Du und N andere". Right after sending the card says "Gemeldet" instead, from the second report of the day with "deine 2. Meldung hier heute", and offers "Nochmal melden" like the tapped card. "Meine Meldungen" on the home sheet lists this device's reports with the bubble's status, a tap flies to the bubble, "Zurückziehen" (asks once) deletes the report. Below the list the account, as far as v00 has one: a name, and "Anderes Gerät verknüpfen" with a 6-character code good for 10 minutes that makes a second phone the same user (its reports come along).

Testing: `make e2e` runs `e2e/run.mjs`, headless Chrome through the flows in `scenarios.md` at three screen sizes with emulated positions. Screenshots in `e2e/out/`. Run it before a commit, add flows for new features.

Map limits: city limit from opendata.heilbronn.de (`src/lib/boundary.json`, rebuild with `make boundary`). Outside the city a grey wash. The screen centre (crosshair in report mode) is clamped to the city polygon (`transformConstrain`), the view itself is free. Min zoom is where the city fills the screen (recomputed on resize and sheet changes), max zoom 18.

Filters (layers button top right, section "Meldungen", `src/lib/filter.ts`): which types, at least 1/3/10 reports, the last 7/14/30 days, only confirmed bubbles. Nothing set shows everything, the choice is kept in localStorage. While a filter is set the layers button has a dot and the home sheet says what in one line ("Zu laut · ab 3 Meldungen · 7 Tage"), a tap on it opens the sheet, the × next to it resets. Types, minimum and confirmed filter on the client: a bubble is counted and coloured by the chosen types only (reports per chosen type summed, at most the bubble's reports), its card shows those types and texts. The days go to the server as `?days=` on the clusters and the bubble's reports request, so bubble ids stay consistent.

Context layers (layers button top right, section "Hintergrund"): UBA road noise map 2022 (raster straight from datahub.uba.de), city tree register (`static/trees.json`, rebuild with `make trees`), bike rides of the last 7 days from the backend, all-time PM2.5 of the Heilbronn senseBox:bikes on a 25 m grid (`static/pm.json`, rebuild with `make pm`, ~5 min of openSenseMap requests). The grid shows as dots below zoom 14.5 and as the real cells above. Thumbnails in the layer list (`static/thumbs/`) are screenshots of the map with that layer on.

`make app` from the repo root. `make build` writes static files to `build/`.

Backend defaults to `http://<same host>:8000`. Override with `VITE_API_URL` in `.env`.
